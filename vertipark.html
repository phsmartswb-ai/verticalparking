The Process

HOW VERTIPARK
WORKS.

From unused land to your car's safest home — four simple steps that make smart parking effortless.

01
🏗️
We Lease Idle Land
We identify unused plots in prime urban zones, lease them from landowners on monthly or yearly agreements, and transform dead real estate into profitable, operational parking towers.
02
🌀
Vertical Rotary System
Our automated rotary carousel system stacks up to 20+ cars vertically on a compact footprint — maximizing land usage by up to 10x compared to traditional parking lots.
03
📲
RFID + App Entry
Each subscriber gets an RFID tag for hands-free access. The Vertipark app lets you request car pickup or drop-off in real time, track slot status and manage subscriptions.
04
🔒
Park & Relax
24/7 CCTV surveillance and on-site security guards ensure your vehicle is always protected. Rain, sun, dust, rodents — none of it reaches your car inside our tower.
