The Honest Math — What Makes Vertipark Actually Profitable
The Tower Economics Are Long-Game. Here's The Path.
💡
Year 1–2.5: Cash-flow negative on CAPEX
The ₹58.5L tower investment takes 2.5–3 years to pay back. This is normal infrastructure. Airlines, hotels, car parks all work this way. Plan for this.
📈
Year 3+ : 60–70% margin
Once CAPEX is retired, your monthly cost drops from ₹81,950 to ~₹65,700. At ₹4,499 avg × 11 slots = ₹49,489 revenue vs ₹65,700 cost. Add day passes → profitable.
🏢
Corporate fleet = your fastest path to profit
One corporate client (IT park, hospital, hotel) booking 4–6 slots at ₹4,499–5,499/slot = ₹18,000–33,000/month guaranteed. This covers your entire land + guard cost from a single client.
🎯
Pre-bookings before launch = survival
Don't open a tower below 70% pre-committed. Every idle slot in Month 1 is a direct cash drain. Sell before you build.
🗺️
Start in premium zones only
Your first 2–3 towers must be in Koramangala, Indiranagar, or Whitefield. Higher land cost, yes — but customers who will pay ₹4,499–5,499 and where demand is proven. Don't start in cheaper zones first.
📊
16-slot towers change the math
If your system supports 16 cars, same land + slightly higher CAPEX but 33% more revenue slots. Break-even per slot drops to ~₹5,750. Pursue 16-slot configurations where possible.
